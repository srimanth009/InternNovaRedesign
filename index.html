FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. If you still need help, reach us at support@internnova.co.in

Is InternNova completely free for students? +
Yes! All InternNova programs, workshops and mentorship sessions are 100% free for eligible students. We believe opportunity should never be gated by financial barriers. There are no registration fees, no subscription charges and no hidden costs at any stage.
Do I need prior experience to apply? +
Not necessarily. Most programs have beginner-friendly tracks that assume no prior professional experience. What we look for is curiosity, commitment and the eagerness to learn. Some advanced tracks may require foundational skills, which are clearly mentioned on the program page.
Will I receive a certificate after completing the internship? +
Yes. Every intern who successfully completes their program and submits their final project receives a digitally verified certificate from InternNova. These certificates are trusted by 200+ companies and can be displayed on LinkedIn, your resume and portfolio sites.
How long are the internship programs? +
Programs range from 4 to 12 weeks depending on the domain and track. Shorter intensive programs focus on specific skill sets while longer programs cover end-to-end project development. All programs are flexible and designed to fit your college schedule.
Are the internships remote or in-office? +
All InternNova internships are 100% remote. You can participate from anywhere in India, at your own pace within the program timeline. We provide all required tools, communication channels and mentor access digitally.
Can I apply if I'm from a non-engineering background? +
Absolutely! We welcome students from all academic backgrounds — arts, commerce, science, management and more. Domains like Digital Marketing, Content Writing, UI/UX Design and Finance are perfect for non-engineers who want to build industry skills.
